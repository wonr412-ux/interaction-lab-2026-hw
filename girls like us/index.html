the vortex

THE VORTEX brings together new writing, conversations and visual works from the family and allies of Mothers and Daughters – a lesbian & trans bar*. Who after eight years of operating as a community project for trans and lesbian people in Brussels and around, is wrapping up. This issue serves as a celebration of Mothers & Daughters, an honouring of those who came before and those who joined us, a vessel for some of our thoughts, an outline of our wild vortex and a gathering place for resources for future trans and lesbian organising in Brussels and beyond.
