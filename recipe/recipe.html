A recipe to play with beads.

How to make beaded jewelry!













step 1

Cut string to be a bit bigger than your wrist






step 2

Pick the beads of your choice (mix and match!)






step 3

Organize beads in desired order






step 4

Thread beads onto string






step 5

Continue threading the beads until it fits around wrist






step 6

Tie the string super tight






step 7

You're done!
